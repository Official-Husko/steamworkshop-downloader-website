What we hold, and for how long

LOOKUPS
The item IDs you resolve are cached for 24 hours to reduce API load. They are not tied to you.
DOWNLOADS
Files pass through and are deleted once the transfer ends. Nothing is archived or mirrored.
LOGS
IP addresses appear in rate-limiting logs, truncated to a /24 and dropped after 7 days.
DONATIONS
Steam keys are encrypted at rest and deleted after redemption. A credits name is stored only if you tick the box, and removed on request. Messages are read once and not published.
COOKIES
None. Recent lookups live in your browser's local storage and never leave the device.
THIRD PARTIES
Requests are made to Steam's API on your behalf. No analytics, no ad networks, no embedded scripts.
Questions or a removal request: privacy@workshop.dl

About

Workshop Downloader resolves published Workshop items through the Steam UGC API and returns their files directly. It exists because keeping a mod after the game is uninstalled, or reading a mod's contents before running it, should not require a client.

Requests are made server-side. The site holds no Steam account of yours, sets no tracking cookies, and keeps no copy of the files after a transfer finishes.

Some games gate Workshop access behind ownership. Coverage for those depends on donated keys, which is what the donation page is for.
